# Adding real food photos

Drop a JPG into this folder named after the item's id and it replaces the
illustration everywhere in the app automatically (menu, home, cart, item sheet).
No code changes, no restart — just refresh.

**Naming:** `<item-id>.jpg` — square photos look best (≥ 600×600, well-lit, tight crop).
